How I Ended Up Loving Mobile Games

Date Published: August 03, 2026 | Author: Lyrra O. Dazal

Introduction

Hi! My name is Lyrra and in this blog, I will be sharing my journey of how I ended up loving mobile games. It all started when I was a child, and my brother introduced me to the world of gaming. I was fascinated by the colorful graphics and engaging gameplay of mobile games, and I quickly became hooked. At first, I was curious about the different types of games available, and I spent hours exploring various genres. But as I explored more, I realized that I'm the type of girl who prefers playing mobile games that are more focused on survival or strategy. I enjoy games that challenge my problem-solving skills and allow me to express my creativity.

One of the games that I particularly enjoy is "Call of Duty Mobile," which is a first-person shooter game that requires quick reflexes and strategic thinking. I also enjoy playing "Mobile Legends," which is a multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) game. And I recently played Genshin Impact, which is an action role-playing game that has a beautiful open world and an engaging storyline. Among the three games, my favorite is Call of Duty Mobile because I love the thrill of the game and the adrenaline rush that comes with it. But playing online games also has negative effects, such as addiction and social isolation. I have learned to balance my gaming habits with other activities, such as spending time with my family and friends, studying, and pursuing my hobbies.

As I continued to play mobile games, I realized that they provided me with a sense of relaxation and stress relief. Whenever I felt overwhelmed or stressed out, I would turn to my favorite mobile games to unwind and escape from reality for a while. Playing mobile games also allowed me to connect with other players from around the world, and I made many new friends through online gaming communities. Overall, my love for mobile games has been a significant part of my life, and I am grateful for the joy and entertainment that they have brought me.
